# Notes for Claude

## Draft2Digital: no public-domain titles (owner's decision, 2026-11-12)

Draft2Digital sent a warning on 2026-11-12 (Tara, Director of Customer
Services): D2D does not publish public-domain works or anything derived from
them, including adaptations, translations, modernized or bilingual editions.
The warning is recorded on the account, and re-uploading or relisting a
blocked title is itself a Terms of Service violation.

The owner has decided: **nothing public-domain goes to Draft2Digital again.**

When working in this repo:
- If the owner asks to upload, list, relist or prepare a title for D2D (or
  Smashwords, which is part of D2D), first check whether it is public domain
  or derived from it. If it is, or might be, remind the owner of this note
  before doing anything, and do not run the D2D scripts for it.
- This covers every book in `C:\git_repo\TKprof_book\books\` and the
  `notes/d2d_metadata_*.json` titles (Dracula, Odyssey, Beowulf, Gilgamesh,
  Scaramouche, Secret Garden, Blue Castle, Tono-Bungay, Enchanted April,
  Richest Man in Babylon, Science of Getting Rich, and the rest): all are
  public-domain derived.
- Only fully original works the owner wrote and owns may go to D2D.
- Other platforms (KDP, Google, Authors Republic, Gumroad) have their own
  rules; this note is about D2D only.

The D2D scripts also show this reminder and ask for confirmation when run
(`draft2digital/_pd_reminder.py`).
