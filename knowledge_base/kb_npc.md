## 1. What is a Net Price Calculator?
A Net Price Calculator (NPC) is a tool that colleges are federally mandated to provide. It estimates the true cost of attendance (tuition, fees, room & board) minus the grants and scholarships a student is likely to receive based on their family's financial situation.

* **Sticker Price**: The published cost of attendance.
* **Net Price**: What you actually pay out of pocket (or via loans).

---

## 2. FAFSA vs. CSS Profile Methodologies

Most colleges use one of two primary forms to calculate financial aid eligibility:

### FAFSA (Federal Methodology)
* **Used By**: Public universities and many private colleges.
* **Key Traits**:
  - Focuses primarily on adjusted gross income (AGI) and liquid assets.
  - Excludes home equity of the primary residence from asset calculations.
  - Does not consider assets or income of non-custodial parents in cases of divorce.
  - Determines eligibility for federal aid (Pell Grants, direct loans) and state-level aid.

### CSS Profile (Institutional Methodology)
* **Used By**: Roughly 300 to 400 top private colleges and universities (e.g., Ivy League schools, Stanford, etc.).
* **Key Traits**:
  - Offers a more comprehensive view of a family's financial health.
  - Often includes home equity of the primary residence in the asset calculations.
  - May require financial information from non-custodial parents.
  - Takes into account medical expenses, private school tuition for siblings, and cost of living.
  - Used to distribute the college's own institutional endowment funds (grants/scholarships).

### The Impact
A family with significant home equity but moderate income might receive generous aid at a FAFSA-only school, but get very little aid at a CSS Profile school that counts home equity heavily. Conversely, families with complex financial situations (e.g. high medical bills) might get better aid adjustments via the CSS Profile.

---

## 3. Common Issues and Tips
* **Outdated Calculators**: Many individual college net price calculators use outdated data (sometimes 2-3 years old), which does not reflect current tuition hikes.
* **Estimate Errors**: Entering incorrect asset values or confusing pre-tax/post-tax income can wildly swing aid estimates.
* **Comparing Offers**: Do not compare sticker prices; compare the actual net prices from award letters. If a CSS Profile school has a higher sticker price, their institutional grants might still make it cheaper than your local state school.
