# Product Copywriting Guidelines

Follow these guidelines when generating marketing copy for the product launches:

## Style & Tone
*   **Tone**: Confident, professional, clear, and value-driven. Avoid generic marketing hype ("game changer", "revolutionary", "groundbreaking").
*   **Perspective**: Talk directly to the target audience, emphasizing tangible benefits rather than listing dry features.
*   **Clarity**: Prefer short, punchy sentences over complex paragraphs.

## Formatting Rules
1.  **Twitter/X Threads**:
    *   Limit to 4 tweets.
    *   Each tweet must be under 240 characters.
    *   Include the application's URL in the first and final tweets.
2.  **LinkedIn Posts**:
    *   Start with an engaging hook line.
    *   Use emojis sparingly to format bullet points.
    *   End with a strong call-to-action (CTA) pointing to the URL.
3.  **Email Newsletter**:
    *   Must start with `Subject:` on its own line.
    *   Include a friendly personalization placeholder (`{{name}}` and/or `{{organization}}`).
4.  **Video Scripts**:
    *   Split into scenes representing pacing for a 60-second video.
    *   Align "Visual" cues precisely with corresponding "Audio" descriptions.

## Video Content & TikTok/Shorts Strategy
1.  **Format & Aspect Ratio**:
    *   Prefer vertical **9:16 (1080x1920)** for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels to maximize mobile reach.
    *   Use clean split-screen layouts (e.g., terminal output on top, code or app preview on bottom) when showing developer workflows.
2.  **Hook & Retention**:
    *   Start the video with an immediate visual or auditory hook in the first 3 seconds focusing on a pain point (e.g., rate limits, errors, or firewalls) rather than a slow introduction.
3.  **Auditory Engagement**:
    *   Avoid hardcoding background music into the output audio file.
    *   Use native platform audio search engines to overlay low-volume trending music tracks directly on top of the clean voiceover to boost algorithmic performance.
4.  **Dev-Log Framing**:
    *   Write scripts in the voice of a developer sharing progress and struggles ("build in public") rather than trying to sell a product. Focus on how technical roadblocks were resolved.
