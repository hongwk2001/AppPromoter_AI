# Monetization Strategy & Traffic Funnel Guide

This document acts as a high-level guide for converting our social media and app promotion work into revenue.

## 💰 1. The Stripe Paywall (Smoke Test)
To validate if users are willing to pay for your tool, set up a paywall early before building complex features.

### Implementation Checklist:
* **The Hook:** Let users upload 1 golf swing video for free to get an instant visual trace of their swing plane.
* **The Paywall:** Blur or lock the detailed coach analysis (e.g., specific posture angles, body rotation speed, correction recommendations).
* **The Payment:** Place an unlocked page behind a **Stripe Payment Link** button: *"Unlock Premium AI Coach Report for $4.99"*.

##  Reddit High-Intent Traffic Funnel
Reddit is our highest-converting source of traffic because users are actively seeking advice.

### Implementation Steps:
1. **Target Subreddits:** Monitor `/r/golfswing`, `/r/golf`, and `/r/GolfInstruction`.
2. **Find Swing Posts:** Look for posts where users ask for feedback on their golf swings.
3. **Run Analysis:** Download the user's video, pass it through SwingPro AI, and generate the trace lines.
4. **Respond with Value:** Reply to the Reddit thread with the trace image and a short explanation, linking back to your app:
   > *"I ran your swing through our AI swing plane tracker. It looks like you're coming over the top by X degrees. Here's your trace report: [App Link]"*
